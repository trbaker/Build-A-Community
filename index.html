About Build a Community

Build a Community is a planning sandbox. Drag housing, services, businesses, transportation, open space, and infrastructure onto a real basemap to design a neighborhood, town, or campus district. Feature categories follow conventional land-use color coding, and the land-use mix panel tracks what you've placed.

Export your design as a PNG image, or save it to ArcGIS Online as a hosted feature layer and web map for further analysis in Map Viewer or National Geographic MapMaker.
